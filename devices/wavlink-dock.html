One shared uplink

Everything behind the dock shares the single 40 Gb/s link to the mini. Run alone, each NVMe drive gets about 3 GB/s. Run together, they split a ceiling of about 4 GB/s.

Why the dock is here

The mini runs one bus-powered NVMe enclosure reliably, not two. On October 19 I tried every arrangement of the two enclosures on the mini's own ports:

SetupResult
Mokin first, ACASIS secondACASIS fell back to USB 10 Gb/s and its drive ran at about 950 MB/s. A new cable and a reboot did not help.
ACASIS first, Mokin secondACASIS came up at 40 Gb/s. The Mokin's drive failed to attach, mounted unwritable, then dropped.
Mokin aloneStable at 40 Gb/s.

Whichever enclosure connected second lost. Each port supplies about 15 W of bus power, and that is the likely cause, though the logs cannot prove it: no PCIe or NVMe errors, and the overcurrent counters stayed at 0. A dock with its own power supply fixes it. Both enclosures now run behind the Wavlink at 40 Gb/s with no drops.

Keep monitors off it

DisplayPort video reserves outbound Thunderbolt bandwidth, the same direction drive writes use. With the KTC monitor (1440p at 180 Hz) on the dock, writes to the NVMe drives capped near 1.5 GB/s. With the monitor on the mini's own port, they reach about 3 GB/s. Reads were barely affected. Both monitors now plug into the mini directly.
