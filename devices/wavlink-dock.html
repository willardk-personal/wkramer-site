Wavlink WL-UTD58 Thunderbolt 5 dock

The one cable that carries almost everything. A self-powered Thunderbolt 5 dock on the mini's port R2, with both drive enclosures on two of its Thunderbolt ports and the front 30 W one charging and syncing an iPad mini or iPhone.

The dock's four USB-A ports are rated 10 Gb/s each, but everything USB shares one 10 Gb/s link inside the dock: the Backup drive, the LAN adapter and the card reader. The front 30 W port is the only 30 W port, so it is the charging spot for the iPad mini or iPhone. With an 80 Gb/s cable the iPad links at 10 Gb/s, its top speed, and its data joins that same shared USB link. The iPad reports a USB-C PD charger offering 5 V / 3 A, 9 V / 3 A and 15 V / 2 A, and it picks 15 V / 2 A, the full 30 W.

One shared uplink

Everything behind the dock shares the single 40 Gb/s link to the mini. Run alone, each NVMe drive reads about 3.8 GB/s. Run together, they split a ceiling of about 4 GB/s.

Why the dock is here

The mini runs one bus-powered NVMe enclosure reliably, not two. On October 19 I tried every arrangement of the two enclosures on the mini's own ports:

SetupResult
Mokin first, ACASIS secondACASIS fell back to USB 10 Gb/s and its drive ran at about 950 MB/s. A new cable and a reboot did not help.
ACASIS first, Mokin secondACASIS came up at 40 Gb/s. The Mokin's drive failed to attach, mounted unwritable, then dropped.
Mokin aloneStable at 40 Gb/s.

Whichever enclosure connected second lost. Each port supplies about 15 W of bus power, and that is the likely cause, though the logs cannot prove it: no PCIe or NVMe errors, and the overcurrent counters stayed at 0. A dock with its own power supply fixes it. Both enclosures run behind the Wavlink at 40 Gb/s with no drops. Only during a one-day trial, November 22 to 23, did DATA run from the mini's own port.

Keep monitors off it

DisplayPort video reserves outbound Thunderbolt bandwidth, the same direction drive writes use. With the main KTC (1440p at 180 Hz) on the dock, Media's writes capped near 1.5 GB/s; with no display on it they reach about 3 GB/s. Reads were barely affected. From November 22 to 23 the main monitor ran through the dock to free a port for DATA. That trial was undone after the screen stayed dark following a restart, cause not found. Both monitors plug into the mini directly.
