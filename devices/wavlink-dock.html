The dock's four USB-A ports are rated 10 Gb/s each, but everything USB shares one 10 Gb/s link inside the dock: the Scratch Disk, the Backup drive, the LAN adapter and the card reader. The front 30 W port is the only 30 W port, so it is the charging spot for the iPad mini or iPhone. With an 80 Gb/s cable the iPad links at 10 Gb/s, its top speed, and its data joins that same shared USB link. The iPad reports a USB-C PD charger offering 5 V / 3 A, 9 V / 3 A and 15 V / 2 A, and it picks 15 V / 2 A, the full 30 W.

One shared uplink

Everything behind the dock shares the single 40 Gb/s link to the mini. Since November 22 that includes the main display. Video travels outbound, the same direction as drive writes, and is reserved first: Media's writes fell from about 3 to 1.5 GB/s, while its reads lost about 5%.

Why the dock is here

The mini runs one bus-powered NVMe enclosure reliably, not two. On October 19 I tried every arrangement of the two enclosures on the mini's own ports:

SetupResult
Mokin first, ACASIS secondACASIS fell back to USB 10 Gb/s and its drive ran at about 950 MB/s. A new cable and a reboot did not help.
ACASIS first, Mokin secondACASIS came up at 40 Gb/s. The Mokin's drive failed to attach, mounted unwritable, then dropped.
Mokin aloneStable at 40 Gb/s.

Whichever enclosure connected second lost. Each port supplies about 15 W of bus power, and that is the likely cause, though the logs cannot prove it: no PCIe or NVMe errors, and the overcurrent counters stayed at 0. A dock with its own power supply fixes it: both enclosures ran behind the Wavlink at 40 Gb/s with no drops until November 22. Now DATA is the one enclosure the mini powers, and Media stays on the dock's power.

Why a monitor is on it

It frees one of the mini's three Thunderbolt ports. DATA took that port and now runs at full speed whatever the dock is doing, so the two NVMe drives together went from about 4.0 GB/s each way to 5.1 write and 7.5 read. The cost is Media's write speed, which matters little for a streaming library. The main screen also goes dark if the dock disconnects.
