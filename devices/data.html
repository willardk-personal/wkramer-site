Thunderbolt 3 against USB4

StandardSpeedIn this setup
Thunderbolt 3 (2015)40 Gb/sThis ACASIS enclosure
USB4 (2019)20 or 40 Gb/sThe Mokin enclosure holding Media
Thunderbolt 4 (2020)40 Gb/sThe mini's three back ports
Thunderbolt 5 (2024)80 or 120 Gb/sThe Wavlink dock, held to 40 Gb/s by the mini

Both enclosures hold a 40 Gb/s link. Inside, the Mokin's USB4 chip reaches its drive at PCIe Gen4, about 3.6 GB/s here. The ACASIS Thunderbolt 3 chip runs Gen3, about 3.2 GB/s, even though the 990 PRO is the faster drive. Neither would get faster on a Thunderbolt 5 port.
