Why it is idle

The mini has its own 2.5 Gb Ethernet port (en0), and that carries all the traffic through the home network: about 2.13 Gb/s down and 2.12 Gb/s up. A second 2.5 Gb port through a shared USB hub would add nothing, so this one stays empty.
