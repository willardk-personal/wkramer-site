Measured speed

Reads measured 89 to 98 MB/s on November 6. That is normal for a portable hard drive, and far below what its 5 Gb/s link could carry. Time Machine blocks ordinary writes, so writes were not tested.

Things to know

  • A hard drive is the slowest device here by a wide margin, which does not matter for hourly backups that run in the background.
  • The SSD BACKUP drive is a second, faster Time Machine destination.
