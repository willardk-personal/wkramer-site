Things to know

  • Keep it on DisplayPort. Through an HDMI 1.4-class USB-C to HDMI cable it dropped to 75 Hz.
  • Keep it off the Wavlink dock. With this monitor on the dock, writes to the NVMe drives behind it capped near 1.5 GB/s. On the mini's own port they reach about 3 GB/s.
  • A second H24T7 sits next to it, also on DisplayPort from its own Thunderbolt port. Both report the same identity to macOS, so tell them apart by port.
  • Its HDMI input carries the MacBook, over a USB-C to HDMI cable from that machine's dock, at 144 Hz. Both computers stay plugged in; the monitor's input button switches between them, no KVM.
