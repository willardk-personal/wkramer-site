Things to know

  • Keep it on DisplayPort. Through an HDMI 1.4-class USB-C to HDMI cable it dropped to 75 Hz.
  • Keep it off the Wavlink dock. With this monitor on the dock, writes to the NVMe drives behind it capped near 1.5 GB/s. On the mini's own port they reach about 3 GB/s.
  • Its white does not match the Dell out of the box: this is a wide-gamut 400-nit panel and the Dell is an sRGB 250-nit one. Match brightness first, then use the sRGB mode, then adjust RGB gain.
