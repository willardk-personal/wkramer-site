Time Machine blocks ordinary writes to its destination, so this drive was tested read-only on an existing file. The bar scale ends at 1,250 MB/s, the ceiling of a 10 Gb/s USB link.

Because it is on the mini's own port, it does not share the dock's link. It held 870 MB/s while all three dock drives ran flat out.

Things to know

  • QLC flash with no DRAM: a large first backup slows once the drive's cache fills. Later backups are small and quick.
  • Keep 15 to 20% free so the drive has room to work.
  • USB enclosures do not pass the drive's temperature or SMART data through, so there is no health readout for it.
