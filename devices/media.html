fio with direct I/O. Random 4k at queue depth 32 (70% read): 56k read and 24k write operations per second.

Short writes land in a fast cache at about 3 GB/s. Once the cache fills, writes settle near 0.9 GB/s, which is the limit of QLC flash. Reads are not affected.

Heat

The enclosure has no fan. The drive sits at 43 to 55 °C in normal use. It reached 70 °C after about 400 GB of nonstop writes, and 77 °C in a stress test that pushed about 560 GB of I/O in 8 minutes. It never throttled. The critical limit is 85 °C. A heatsink or some airflow would help on very long runs.
