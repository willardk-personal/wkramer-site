fio with direct I/O. Random 4k at queue depth 32 (70% read): 55k read and 23k write operations per second with the drive cool (November 22).

Short writes land in a fast cache, which takes about 3 GB/s. Behind the dock with the display on it, they arrive at no more than about 1.5 GB/s. Once the cache fills, writes settle near 0.9 GB/s, which is the limit of QLC flash. Reads are not affected.

Heat

The enclosure has no fan. The drive sits at 43 to 55 °C in normal use. It reached 70 °C after about 400 GB of nonstop writes, and 77 °C in a stress test that pushed about 560 GB of I/O in 8 minutes. It never throttled. In the November 22 swap test it went from 49 to 58 °C, and the longer retest after it peaked at 77 °C, with no errors or drops. The critical limit is 85 °C. A heatsink or some airflow would help on very long runs.
