fio with direct I/O. The bar scale ends at 1,250 MB/s, the ceiling of a 10 Gb/s USB link. Random 4k at queue depth 32: 12k read and 5k write operations per second on either port. The USB-A port costs about 3% on writes and 9% on reads, from the two extra hub hops.

The drive is limited by its USB bridge, not by the dock. It keeps full speed while the two NVMe drives are busy, because 0.8 GB/s is a small share of the dock's 4 GB/s.

History

This SSD used to sit in the M.2 slot of an Ivanky USB dock, where it measured the same ~780 MB/s. When the Wavlink replaced that dock, the drive moved into its own enclosure.
