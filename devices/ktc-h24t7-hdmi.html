The same panel as the main display: 24-inch Fast IPS, 2560×1440, 400 nits, about 95% DCI-P3.

Things to know

  • Keep it on DisplayPort. Through an HDMI 1.4-class USB-C to HDMI cable it dropped to 75 Hz.
  • Its HDMI input carries the MacBook, over a USB-C to HDMI cable from that machine's dock, at 144 Hz. That is plenty for work. Both computers stay plugged in; the monitor's input button switches between them, no KVM.
  • All three of the mini's back Thunderbolt ports are in use: this display on R1, the main display on R4 and the dock on R2.
