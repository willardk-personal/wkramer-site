Worth remembering

Eject before unplugging

Unsafe shutdowns on record: P310 56, 990 PRO 70, internal 11. All drives report no data errors.

Video is king: a dock does video or fast drives

Display traffic is reserved first on the Thunderbolt link. With a monitor on this dock, drive writes fell from about 3 GB/s to 1.5 GB/s. So the dock carries the SSDs and both monitors plug into the mini. A one-day trial on November 22 put the main monitor on the dock; it was undone the next day. The MacBook makes the opposite choice.

Two enclosures need the dock

The mini runs only one bus-powered NVMe enclosure reliably. The Wavlink's own power supply lets both run at 40 Gb/s.

No spare lane

DATA and Media rarely transfer at the same time, so sharing the dock is fine. All three back Thunderbolt ports are taken (two monitors and the dock), so neither drive can get a port of its own.
