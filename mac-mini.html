Worth remembering

Eject before unplugging

Unsafe shutdowns on record: P310 47, 990 PRO 62, internal 10. All drives report no data errors.

Monitors stay off the dock

A display on the dock takes outbound Thunderbolt bandwidth, the direction drive writes use. Writes fell from about 3 GB/s to 1.5 GB/s.

Two enclosures need the dock

The mini runs only one bus-powered NVMe enclosure reliably. The Wavlink's own power supply lets both run at 40 Gb/s.

R2 is the spare lane

DATA and Media rarely transfer at the same time. If both ever need full speed at once, move one to the free R2 port.
