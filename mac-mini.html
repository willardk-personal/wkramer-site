Worth remembering

Eject before unplugging

Unsafe shutdowns on record: P310 52, 990 PRO 68, internal 11. All drives report no data errors.

Video is king: a dock does video or fast drives

Display traffic is reserved first on the Thunderbolt link. With a monitor on this dock, drive writes fell from about 3 GB/s to 1.5 GB/s. So the dock carries the SSDs and both monitors plug into the mini. The MacBook makes the opposite choice.

Two enclosures need the dock

The mini runs only one bus-powered NVMe enclosure reliably. The Wavlink's own power supply lets both run at 40 Gb/s.

R2 is the spare lane

DATA and Media rarely transfer at the same time. If both ever need full speed at once, move one to the free R2 port.
