How they are connected

The dock has one HDMI 2.1 and one DisplayPort 2.1 output, and the box says they cannot be used at the same time. So one display runs from the dock's video port and the other takes a different path. macOS lists displays under the graphics chip, not under Thunderbolt, so it does not report which display is on which path.

Sharing with the Mac mini

The Mac mini drives the same two monitors on their other inputs: the KTC over DisplayPort at 180 Hz and the Dell over HDMI at 100 Hz. Switching a monitor's input moves it from one computer to the other.
