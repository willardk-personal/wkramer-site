macOS lists displays under the graphics chip, not under Thunderbolt, so it shows the dock's ports as empty with both monitors on them. The read shows the video leaving the MacBook over the dock cable at HBR3, and the dock's own HDMI / DisplayPort output unused. The MacBook's own HDMI port is free too.

Sharing with the Mac mini

Both computers stay connected to both monitors all the time: the Mac mini on each monitor's DisplayPort input at 180 Hz with VRR, the MacBook on its HDMI input at 144 Hz. There is no KVM switch. Switching a monitor's input moves it from one computer to the other, and nothing has to be unplugged.

144 Hz is the KTC's HDMI limit. The MacBook is for work, not games, so it loses nothing that matters.
