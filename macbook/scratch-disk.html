The drive

DriveKingSpec 512 GB M.2 NVMe, PCIe Gen3 x4, 2280
WhereThe Ivanky dock's built-in M.2 slot, whose Realtek RTL9210B-CG bridge (0bda:9210) sits right on the dock's 10 Gb/s VIA VL822 hub
LinkUSB 10 Gb/s, shared with everything else on the dock. With the slot empty the bridge showed 480 Mb/s.
VolumeScratch Disk (APFS). 30 GiB used of 477 GiB (November 27). SMART isn't readable through the USB bridge.
JobClaude's temporary files and working copies on the MacBook, so the internal SSD stays clear.
BeforeUntil November 27 it was the Mac mini's Scratch Disk, in a Ugreen USB enclosure on the Wavlink dock. Earlier still it sat in this same Ivanky slot when the dock was under the mini.

The MacBook run wrote a 4 GiB file flushed with F_FULLFSYNC, then read it with the page cache bypassed. The Mac mini runs used fio with direct I/O, so they are close but not identical tests. The bar scale ends at 1,250 MB/s, the ceiling of a 10 Gb/s USB link. A second, identical KingSpec arrives November 28 to compare the M.2 slot against a 10 Gb/s enclosure, both on a MacBook port and on the dock.
