Measured November 8, 2026. On Wi-Fi, responsiveness under load was low (511 ms). The Mac mini, on its own Ethernet port, measured about 2.1 Gb/s each way the same day.

Wired through the dock is about nine times faster than Wi-Fi here. The download figure sits below the Mac mini's because the dock's Ethernet rides a USB adapter that shares one uplink with the dock's other USB devices.
