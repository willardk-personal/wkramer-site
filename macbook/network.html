macOS networkQuality. The November 27 runs used -s, which tests download and upload one after the other; runs without it test both at once and show a lower upload. The 37.4 ms idle ping was one run; the other on that port read 10.0 ms.

Why download stops near 1.74 Gb/s

The adapter, not the dock. It downloads the same on the MacBook's own port as on the Ivanky, while the Mac mini on the same switch gets 2.30 Gb/s. Upload is close to the mini's. It's the same Realtek RTL8156 chip that held the Mac mini to about 1.85 Gb/s receiving when it was tried on a USB adapter on November 12; only the mini's built-in port receives at full rate.

Link to the Mac mini

The two Macs normally talk over the home network, now both wired to the same switch. In a test on November 25 a direct Thunderbolt link, with the MacBook on the Mac mini's Wavlink dock, ran at 37.7 Gb/s each way, about 16 times the home network's 2.5 Gb/s.
