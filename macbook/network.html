The wired tests used each Thunderbolt dock's built-in Realtek RTL8156 2.5 GbE adapter. Wi-Fi on 6 GHz now gets close to what wired did through those docks.

Link to the Mac mini

The two Macs normally talk over the home network. In a test on November 25 a direct Thunderbolt link, with the MacBook on the Mac mini's Wavlink dock, ran at 37.7 Gb/s each way, about 16 times the home network's 2.5 Gb/s.
