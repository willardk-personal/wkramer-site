One hub behind every USB port

Inside the dock, every USB port, the 2.5 Gb Ethernet and the card reader share one 10 Gb/s USB 3 uplink. That is a small slice of the 80 Gb/s cable, and it is the real limit for anything plugged into the dock's USB sockets.

It is why the SanDisk backup SSD moved off the dock to its own MacBook port: its backups no longer share that uplink with the wired network, and it runs faster in both directions.
