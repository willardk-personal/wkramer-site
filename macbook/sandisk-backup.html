8 GiB sequential, three runs each, write includes sync, read after a cache purge, no backup running. The own-port figures are the middle of 842 to 847 write and 812 to 814 read. The bar scale ends at 1,250 MB/s, the ceiling of a 10 Gb/s USB link.

The drive is faster on its own port in both directions, and its backups no longer compete with the wired network for the dock's single USB uplink.
