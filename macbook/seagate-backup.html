Things to know

  • Shingled drives write quickly in bursts and slow down on long rewrites. Time Machine's hourly backups suit that well.
  • It shares the dock's USB uplink with the wired network. At hard drive speeds that does not matter, which is why this one stayed on the dock and the SanDisk SSD moved off.
