About

I graduated from Stuyvesant High School in 1982, then studied electrical engineering at Columbia University's School of Engineering, earning a BS in 1986 and an MS in 1988.

For the past 27 years I have worked at the SIFMA Foundation. Away from work I build and tune my own computing setup, and I write up what I learn here.
