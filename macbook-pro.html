Worth remembering

The dock's USB is one 10 Gb/s lane

Every USB socket, the Ethernet port and the card reader share it. Fast drives belong on the MacBook's own ports or the dock's Thunderbolt ports.

HDMI or DisplayPort, not both

The dock's two video outputs cannot be used at the same time, so the Dell takes HDMI and the KTC runs from a Thunderbolt 5 port.

Video is king: a dock does video or fast drives

On any dock, display traffic is reserved first and data gets what is left. This dock carries both monitors, so the fast drive sits on the MacBook's own port, where it measured quicker. The Mac mini makes the opposite choice: its dock carries the SSDs and the monitors plug into the mini.

One free Thunderbolt 5 port

The KTC uses one of the dock's two downstream ports. The other is empty, rated up to 120 Gb/s.

What moved to the Mac mini

The Wavlink dock and the Samsung 990 PRO both live on the Mac mini now. The ACASIS enclosure that held the 990 PRO is a spare.
