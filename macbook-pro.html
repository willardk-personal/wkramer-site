The 990 PRO figure is an 8 GB test with caching bypassed and a forced flush, run while a Time Machine backup was copying over the same dock. This is a different method from the one on the Mac mini page, so compare numbers only within one method. The read is single-threaded and understates the drive.

A drive that moves around

  • The ACASIS enclosure with the 990 PRO lived on the Mac mini for a while. A Thunderbolt 3 chip inside it, plus a USB fallback, made it drop to USB speed when the mini couldn't power two enclosures at once.
  • On this dock, which is powered separately, it holds 40 Gb/s. The full story is in the Mac mini troubleshooting notes.
  • A Thunderbolt 5 port doesn't make it faster. The enclosure's Thunderbolt 3 chip and its link both top out at 40 Gb/s.

Thunderbolt and memory

  • Bus 0 runs at 80 Gb/s to the dock, out of a 120 Gb/s maximum for the port. Buses 1 and 2 are free.
  • On the Mac mini side, a cable between the two Macs reached about 2.9 GB/s using parallel streams. The link isn't connected now.
  • Memory at the time of reading: about 20.9 GiB active, 20.9 GiB inactive, 3.6 GiB wired, no swap.
